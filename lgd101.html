LGD101

Introduction to Local Government

100 Level • First Semester

Course Overview

This course introduces students to the concept, evolution, structure, functions and importance of Local Government Administration. Students will understand the historical development of local government in Nigeria, its constitutional roles and its significance in grassroots development.
