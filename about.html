About L-TUTOR

Empowering students of the Department of Local Government Administration and Development Studies through modern digital learning.

What is L-TUTOR?

L-TUTOR is an academic learning platform specially developed for students of the Department of Local Government Administration and Development Studies. It provides easy access to lecture notes, books, past questions, announcements, videos and other educational resources anytime and anywhere.

Our Mission

To improve teaching and learning by providing students with reliable academic materials, simplifying communication between lecturers and students, and encouraging independent learning.

Our Vision

To become the leading digital learning platform for Local Government Administration students while promoting academic excellence through technology.

Why L-TUTOR?

Students often struggle to obtain lecture materials, books and past questions. L-TUTOR brings everything together in one secure platform, making studying faster, easier and more organized.
